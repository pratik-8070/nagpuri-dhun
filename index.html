Quick guide

जानकारी
What is Nagpuri Dhun?

A browser-based always-on radio concept for Jharkhand's Sadri and Nagpuri music, rotating mood by time of day — no app, no login.

Why don't the songs play yet?

Every track's audioUrl is left empty on purpose — this is a template. Add your own licensed audio source to hear playback.

How is the rotation decided?

It's computed client-side from the current time in the Asia/Kolkata timezone and mapped to one of four moods: dawn, afternoon, evening, night.
