Quick guide

जानकारी
What is Nagpuri Dhun?

A browser-based always-on radio concept for Jharkhand's Sadri and Nagpuri music, rotating mood by time of day — no app, no login.

Where does the audio actually come from?

Each track streams from YouTube via the official IFrame Player API, embedded off-screen — this site's controls just drive that hidden player. If a video's owner ever disables embedding or removes it, that track will show a short error instead of failing silently.

How is the rotation decided?

It's computed client-side from the current time in the Asia/Kolkata timezone and mapped to one of four moods: dawn, afternoon, evening, night.
